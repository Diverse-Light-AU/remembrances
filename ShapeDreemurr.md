# Who Was ShapeDreemurr?
### A memorial investigation

*Written 15/01/2026 (DD/MM/YYYY). Revised with additional sources and corrections.*

> **Note:** Some things have changed since this was first written, so not everything here may still be up to date. Worlds, statuses, and server listings can change or disappear over time.

*Much of the groundwork for this memorial came from **CharaTBZ**, who learned alongside me and helped explore many of the VRChat worlds referenced here, whenever their headset cooperated long enough to let them.*

---

## Introduction

Over 20 hours in VRChat and several weeks in the Chara Gaming Discord server, one name kept coming up: **ShapeDreemurr**. Most people recognized it. Few could explain it.

I should be upfront that I'm a newcomer to this community. Shape was known mainly to the older members, some of whom knew her personally and some only by name. For those of us who arrived later, she was a name attached to a loss, with very little else.

Before anything else: my deepest condolences to Shape's family, friends, and everyone who knew her. This project is not about attention, recognition, or profit. Its only purpose is to preserve a legacy and make sure she isn't forgotten.

---

## Setting Aside Assumptions

Because she passed in 2021, many people assume COVID-19 was the cause. I assumed the same at first. Without evidence, that assumption has to be set aside, and as the later sections show, it turned out to be wrong.

## Publicly Available Information

Google returned little of use, so I searched with Startpage instead. That turned up two memorial videos by the same YouTuber:

- [Goodbye ShapeDreemurr (R.I.P. Tribute)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IoyR-Twpa_0)
- [ShapeDreemurr will never be forgotten](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bPyZGxCDPjY)

According to their descriptions, ShapeDreemurr was a young VRChat player who passed away on **July 29th, 2021**, at **15 years old**. At least one video was recorded in a memorial world built by her boyfriend for her friends and community.

That world still exists:

- [ShapeDreemurr's memorial (VRChat world by JoshTMW), VRCList](https://vrclist.com/world/45794)
- [ShapeDreemurr's memorial, VRCW world details](https://en.vrcw.net/world/detail/wrld_74745192-8f10-4390-98e1-59cc67ab9fd4)

Its description reads: *"R.I.P ShapeDreemurr. They wanted to make an avatar world, but never could. So I put her avatars in here for her. God speed my love..."*

Other public tributes:

- [Tribute post on X, August 5th, 2021](https://x.com/STARBOYWHISKEY/status/1423356680822214659)
- [Memorial tribute on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@marlaharris43/video/7545067917407309087)

From these, a few things are clear. Shape was a girl, she died very young, she was a big Undertale fan (hence "Dreemurr"), and she left a real mark on the people around her. She also made genuinely great art.

## Traces Left Behind

Community members pointed me toward two circles where much of Shape's history still lives: **Undernet** and **Lotus's circles**.

- [UNDERNET Discord server](https://discord.com/servers/undernet----797194968221089822)
- [Undernet HUB world, VRCList](https://vrclist.com/world/2864)
- [Undernet VRChat community, VRCList](https://vrclist.com/community/37)

Two people came up again and again as having known her personally: **Virus/Barrel** (one person, known by both names) and **JoshTMW**. Josh's connection was consistently described as the closest, and he built the memorial world above.

Both are still active, but mostly within their own circles and possibly Undernet. I only noticed this because their statuses still read "RIP ShapeDreemurr," unchanged for more than four years. That kind of persistence says more than any explanation could.

One caution came up during these conversations, and it's worth repeating: the way people are remembered can shift after they're gone. When something was said matters as much as what was said.

## Not Truly Open Source

Some of Shape's history only exists inside VRChat worlds, and not everyone can reach them. Heavier worlds need hardware powerful enough to render them. The memory is public in principle but gated by performance in practice.

To work around this, I ran the investigation in Friends+ instances so friends, and their friends, could join, observe, and help. These worlds aren't reliable "open-source intelligence," but they are memorials in their own right.

## Discordant Knowledge

What I found wasn't a single story. It was fragments spread across Discord servers, VRChat worlds, profile statuses, old conversations, and the memories of people who no longer post much.

Worlds associated with her history include **Undernet**, **Lotus**, **Snowdin HD 2.0**, and a Snowdin-style world where interacting with a tombstone leads to a memorial.

Several world creators are likely sources of further memorials, including **Unidelta**, **DriscollGamer**, and **4Bakers**. The Undernet hub world lists many more. If a name appears there, that person is probably a world creator, and there's a good chance a memorial to Shape exists somewhere in their work.

Other traces: JoshTMW's profile art shows his OC beside a translucent, haloed, winged version of Shape's OC. There's an emoji called *Shapesoul*. There are recurring colors and angelic imagery tied to her. None of these are facts on their own, but together they show she left a recognizable imprint.

When newer members ask "who's Shape Dreemurr?", the answers are hesitant. Not because she didn't matter, but because memory doesn't preserve itself.

## Answered, Gently

The silence finally broke through a memorial left by someone who loved her. What follows comes from that, not from rumor.

Shape was born on **December 2nd, 2005**. She was autistic and was bullied for years, which made school a hard place to be, and she struggled with depression.

As a child she needed a liver transplant. It succeeded, but the medication that followed permanently weakened her immune system.

She found VRChat and its Undertale community, where she was known for her creativity and warmth rather than her medical history. She made art, helped design avatars, and made people feel seen.

In spring 2020 she met JoshTMW in Snowdin HD. They grew close through conversation, collaboration, birthday surprises, and long talks. For a while she seemed lighter.

In July 2021 she was diagnosed with **PTLD** (post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorder), a cancer linked to immune suppression. Treatment started right away and she was responding well.

Then she suffered a severe brain hemorrhage. Her platelet count was too low for surgery, and medication didn't help. Survival would have meant permanent brain damage and life on machines.

**Shape passed away on July 29th, 2021.**

Her death wasn't sudden in the way rumors suggest. It was the end of a long, unfair chain of medical problems no 15-year-old should have to face. Through all of it, she kept loving people and being loved.

What remains: memorial worlds across VRChat, a Hall of Souls, status messages that haven't changed in years, the *Shapesoul* emoji, and, notably, her removal from places like the Undernet Hub during later revamps. Digital spaces don't remember anyone unless someone chooses to.
